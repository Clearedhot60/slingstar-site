Slingstar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Slingstar is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no login and no chat. This page explains what information is used when you play.

What the game stores

Your best score, stars, upgrades, unlocked comets, missions and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Slingstar never shows an ad on its own. The only ads are rewarded videos that you choose to watch, by tapping "Continue", "Double stars" or "Free stars". Those ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, other device identifiers, IP address (which indicates your general location), device and operating system type, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

When the game first opens, your iPhone asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In regions where the law requires it, the game also asks for your consent to personalised advertising, and you can change that choice by reinstalling the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Leaderboard

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score and Game Center nickname appear on the worldwide leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can sign out of it in your iPhone's Settings.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not run our own servers for this game and we do not receive personal information about players.

Children

Slingstar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
