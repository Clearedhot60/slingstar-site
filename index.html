Purchases

Star packs are sold through Apple. If a purchase did not arrive, force-close the game and open it again: Apple re-delivers unfinished purchases on launch. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Slingstar never plays an ad on its own. The only ads are the ones you choose, by tapping "Continue", "Double stars" or "Free stars". If an ad does not load, wait a moment and try again; nothing is lost.

Sound

The game follows your iPhone's ring/silent switch. Music and sound each have their own volume slider under Settings, from the menu or the pause screen.
